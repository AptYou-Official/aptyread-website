## Current delivery

It is linked from the dashboard; the earlier three lightweight audio previews remain available.

The route is not the complete level and does not establish literacy effectiveness.

## Shared app contract

- Child-operated by default: instructions, answer help, stopping, resuming and retry need no adult judgement or login.
- Keep neutral instruction replay distinct from answer-model help. When instruction audio itself is intended input, such as A18, it is not answer help.
- Save screen exposure, actual audio start/end, first response, subsequent supported response, choices shown and help separately. Record audio failure as technical failure. Reaching the end or skipping is not mastery.
- An incorrect first response gets a model and one optional supported retry. Continue/finish stays available. No forced correct-answer loop.
- The first episode's meaning task supplies W01 intentionally. It is listening-supported meaning, not unaided reading. Guided construction retains the visible word model and is not unrestricted spelling.
- Later retrieval must precede recap audio and answer-bearing scenes. Dashboard exposure and an immediately preceding lesson can affect the interpretation; a new lesson ID does not create delayed retention.
- Store the actual content version and save position under separate lesson keys. Old preview positions are not evidence of completed teaching prerequisites.
- Handwriting is practice unless independently observed. Never award correctness from a Done button, elapsed time, a tracing endpoint or a spoken try without observation.
- Formation media and the screen guide must share an approved movement reference. Until then, do not claim the writing route is delivered.

## Before expanding the next batch

Review E01 with the founder/educator, including whether the floor and head pictures are immediately understandable. Note whether children understand a spoken instruction, use replay/help, find Continue/Stop and return without coaching. Record what explanation an adult actually supplies.

On a later occasion, observe a small amount of word reading before the answer model, plus meaning and appropriately labelled writing attempts. Keep usability observations separate from learning outcomes. Use these observations to revise E02/E03 and the next batch; no claim of a proven course follows from a successful preview.

Next implementation: finish reviewed E01 writing assets when supplied, extend the reusable activity/persistence patterns to E02, then E03 and its optional dictation. Do not bulk-record C-E until their exact lesson scripts are reconciled.

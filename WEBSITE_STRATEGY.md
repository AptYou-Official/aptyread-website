### **Section 4: How Learning Works** (Classroom Pedagogy)

**Headline:** "Every Lesson is Like a Real Classroom"

**Example: A Complete Letter Lesson (12 Topics)**

For each letter, your child goes through a comprehensive learning journey:

1. **Meet the Letter** → Video introduces the letter and its sound
2. **Spot the Letter** → Activity: Recognize big & small letters (4-level recognition)
3. **Make the Sound** → Video: Mouth & tongue placement instruction
4. **Practice the Sound** → Activity: Sound checking practice
5. **Letter Words** → Video: Introduces 3 words starting with the letter
6. **Say the Words** → Activity: AI pronunciation feedback (Speech-to-Text)
7. **Hear & Find** → Activity: Listen and identify correct words
8. **Write Big Letter** → Video: Formation instruction for uppercase
9. **Draw Big Letter** → Activity: Trace on screen, draw on paper, or skip
10. **Write Little Letter** → Video: Formation instruction for lowercase
11. **Draw Little Letter** → Activity: Tracing/writing practice
12. **Letter Star** → Video: Celebration & review

**Review Lessons (After Every 3 Letters):**
- Sound Match: Match sounds to letters
- Find the Letter: Identify letters from mixed options
- Word Wizard: Recognize words by their starting sounds
- Comprehensive practice across 3 letters

**Mastery Requirement:**
- Must achieve 70% in review lessons to proceed
- Final assessment (2 lessons) tests all letters learned

**What Makes It Different:**
- "Multi-modal learning: See, hear, speak, write"
- "AI-powered pronunciation feedback"
- "Proper mouth placement instruction for ESL learners"
- "Comprehensive coverage: Every letter gets 12 focused topics"
- "Engaging, not addictive"
- "Learning > dopamine"
- "No excessive gamification"
- "Real activities, not mindless tapping"

## 🎯 Image Style Guidelines

### DO:
- ✅ Real, authentic moments (not overly staged)
- ✅ Diverse children (different ethnicities, ages)
- ✅ Natural, warm lighting
- ✅ Genuine expressions (happy, focused, confident)
- ✅ Real-world contexts (home, library, school, outdoors)
- ✅ Parent-child interactions (when appropriate)
- ✅ Focus on reading/learning moments

### DON'T:
- ❌ Overly posed or staged
- ❌ Generic stock photos that feel fake
- ❌ Too many screens/devices (we're showing real reading, not screen time)
- ❌ Stressed or pressured children
- ❌ Too gamified or flashy (keep it calm, respectful)

## 🎨 Technical Specifications

### File Formats:
- **Photos:** JPG (for photos with many colors)
- **Graphics/Illustrations:** PNG (for transparency) or SVG (for logos/icons)
- **File Size:** Optimize for web (under 500KB for large images, under 200KB for smaller)

### Dimensions:
- **Hero/Large Images:** 1200px-1600px width (height varies)
- **Section Images:** 800px-1200px width
- **Small/Accent Images:** 400px-600px width
- **Always maintain aspect ratio**

### Optimization:
- Use Next.js Image component (already integrated)
- Images will be automatically optimized
- Use `priority={true}` only for above-fold images

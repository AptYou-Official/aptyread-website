# Mobile Design Issues & Standard Patterns

## Common "Not Standard Feeling" Issues on Mobile:

1. **Text Alignment** - Centered text on mobile can feel off (left-aligned is more standard)
2. **Spacing** - Inconsistent or awkward gaps between elements
3. **Typography Scale** - Text sizes don't feel natural
4. **Touch Targets** - Buttons/text areas too small or too large
5. **Visual Balance** - Elements don't feel properly weighted
6. **Padding/Margins** - Not enough breathing room or too much
7. **Line Heights** - Text feels cramped or too spaced out
8. **Element Proportions** - Things feel out of scale

## Standard Mobile Patterns:

- **Text**: Usually left-aligned (centered only for headings/CTAs)
- **Spacing**: Consistent rhythm (8px, 16px, 24px, 32px)
- **Buttons**: Full-width or appropriately sized with good padding
- **Typography**: Clear hierarchy, readable sizes
- **Padding**: Generous but not excessive (16px-24px typical)
- **Line Height**: 1.5-1.6 for body text
- **Touch Targets**: Minimum 44px height

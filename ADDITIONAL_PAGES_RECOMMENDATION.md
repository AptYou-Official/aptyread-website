## 🎯 High Priority Pages (Recommended First)

### 1. **FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)** ⭐⭐⭐
**Route:** `/faq` or `/faq.html`

**Why Essential:**
- Parents have many questions before enrolling their child
- Reduces support emails
- Builds trust through transparency
- SEO benefits (people search for "how does AptyRead work?")

**Suggested Sections:**
- **Getting Started:**
  - How do I start? (Try 4 free lessons)
  - Do I need to register? (No, try without registration)
  - What age is this for? (3-10 years)
  - Is this only for English? (Currently English, more coming)
  
- **Learning & Progress:**
  - How long does each lesson take? (~30 minutes)
  - Can my child do multiple lessons per day? (1 per day default, max 2 if parent sets)
  - What if my child struggles? (Mastery system - must pass 70% to continue)
  - Can my child review lessons? (Yes, unlimited review of completed lessons)
  
- **Pricing & Payments:**
  - How much does it cost? (Level-by-level pricing)
  - Is there a subscription? (No, pay only for what you need)
  - Can I try before paying? (Yes, 4 free lessons)
  - What if I don't like it? (No subscription to cancel, just don't enroll next level)
  - What payment methods? (Through App Store/Play Store)
  
- **Technical:**
  - What devices does it work on? (Android/iPhone)
  - Does it work offline? (Mostly, but needs internet for new lessons)
  - Can multiple children use one account? (Yes, multiple child profiles)
  
- **Child Safety & Screen Time:**
  - How much screen time? (One lesson per day, ~30 minutes)
  - Is it safe for children? (No ads, no external links, child-safe)
  - Do you collect children's data? (See Privacy Policy - minimal data)
